Why choose PlanIt?

01

Plan ahead with confidence

PlanIt gives you a clear view of your degree before registration begins. See how courses fit together, understand what needs to be taken first, and identify potential problems before they affect your semester

02

Be ready for change

Plans do not always go as expected. When a course is unavailable or your plans change, PlanIt helps you explore alternative paths. Build your plan visually by draging your courses into the semester you want to take them.

03

Know what works

Instead of guessing whether your plan is possible, PlanIt checks prerequisites, corequisites, course availability, and credit limits as you build it. You can make changes with confidence and see the impact immediately
