How to Plan

1. Browse your available courses in the sidebar on the left.

2. Drag a course into the semester you want to take it.

3. If a course needs a prerequisite or year standing you haven't met yet, we'll let you know.

4. Use "+ Add Semester" if you need an extra term, like a summer session.
